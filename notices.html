Signing and release status

The complete Windows installer, uninstaller, two project-owned native executables and bundled command plugin use the same AldoApps self-signed beta certificate. This provides a consistent signing identity that can be compared with the published certificate and file hashes. Windows does not automatically trust independently self-signed publishers, so it may display an unknown-publisher or untrusted-root message. That describes certificate-authority trust; it does not indicate that the application is a preview or incomplete. No trust-store import or operating-system security change is required by these instructions.

The complete Mac application, nested tools and installer use Apple Developer ID distribution identities, hardened runtime and trusted timestamps. Apple accepted this exact published package for notarization and its ticket is stapled, allowing normal Gatekeeper verification. The sister XPPanelsVR application for Apple Vision Pro has also been accepted by Apple. Notarization and sister-app acceptance do not imply endorsement of the separate Quest edition.

The current direct Quest APK is the production-signed, non-debuggable ARM64 build 0.2.0-beta.2-panel-fixes, code 20019, for Quest 3/3S. It shares the signing identity of the separate private Meta Beta channel. A newer production-signed APK can update an earlier production build in place; publishing a direct APK does not update Meta’s channel. Meta channel membership shares the tester’s Meta email address and alias with AldoApps and permits release-channel email. Older development-signed APKs remain archived; changing from that legacy signing identity requires an intentional uninstall that can erase app-local settings.

Local app processing and network boundary

The Mac and Windows Companions capture selected simulator instrument windows. Outside view, X-Plane process audio and ISCS sharing are separate controls. Audio does not capture the microphone or the general desktop mix. ISCS touches use the explicitly selected, foreground, uncovered simulator window. Local control requests are bounded and require a fresh armed session.

The normal connection uses USB forwarding to services on the computer’s local loopback interface. The optional Wi-Fi fallback is designed only for a trusted private LAN approved by the user. Simulator UDP and local HTTP intentionally use this local trust boundary instead of an internet-facing encrypted pairing service; no port forwarding, public address or cloud relay is needed. Keep the services on USB or your trusted private network and never expose their ports to the internet. The app adds no analytics, advertising or automatic diagnostic uploader. Website downloads are currently public and do not require an email account. Local settings and logs remain on your computer; support sharing is your choice.

This website and downloads

The site and public downloads are hosted by GitHub. GitHub handles ordinary hosting request information, including IP addresses, under its privacy statement and maintains aggregate release download counts.

Reading this site and downloading its files remain public; no email or account is required. When you activate a download link, a small anonymous request to Cloudflare counts the link request by UTC day and hour, file/version, product, platform and approximate country. Loading the public landing page also sends one anonymous page-view event per document load, aggregated by UTC day and hour, product and approximate country. Repeat loads count again; these are page views, not unique visitors. Only the landing page (including its index.html address) is counted; other pages, query strings, URL fragments and referrers are not included in the page-view event. Page-view history begins when this tracking is activated; earlier visits are not reconstructed. The aggregate table stores no email, account identifier, raw IP, user-agent, fingerprint or visitor identifier. It uses no tracking cookies. This counts link requests, not completed transfers, installations or unique people. Repeated clicks and some bots may count; direct GitHub downloads and failed tracking are not included. VPNs/proxies can affect country. Tracking is independent of the download: blocked requests, outages or free-plan limits do not prevent you from downloading.

The owner can inspect the aggregates privately and receive a private daily email through Google Apps Script. Only aggregate page-view, download-link and public GitHub counter statistics are included; page views alone do not trigger an email. Aggregates remain until deleted. Cloudflare may process normal connection/security information under its privacy policy. Optional Worker request logging is disabled; this does not mean Cloudflare keeps no infrastructure or security logs. The site has no advertising or tracking cookies added by AldoApps, and does not load external fonts or icon services.

The demonstration video is embedded only after you press play. Before that click the site makes no YouTube video request. Playing it connects to the youtube-nocookie.com player and is subject to Google/YouTube privacy practices. The direct YouTube link also leaves this site.

Discord invitations open the XPPanels community on Discord. Joining and posting are optional and follow Discord's privacy policy. No Discord embed loads on this website, and you do not need to join Discord to download the apps.
